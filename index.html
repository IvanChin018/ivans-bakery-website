Our Story

A Tradition of
Artisan Baking

Founded in 2018 by master baker Marie Laurent, La Mie Dorée was born from a simple belief: that bread should be more than sustenance — it should be an experience.


Every loaf begins its journey at 4 AM, when our bakers gather in the warm glow of our ovens. We use only stone-milled organic flour, natural starters cultivated over years, and pure spring water. No shortcuts, no preservatives — just patience, skill, and passion.
